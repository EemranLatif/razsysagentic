==========================================================

A simple FAQ/services contract between RazSupport Ltd and RazBank. Covers scope of services, payment terms, liability, and termination only. No employment clauses, no IP, no data handling.
Expected planner output → ["faq"] → 1 agent runs


my_contract.pdf — Multi-domain (Employment & Consultancy Agreement)
=================================================================
A full engagement contract with three distinct parts:

Part A — Employment terms, 24-month non-compete, 90-day notice, garden leave
Part B — Full IP assignment including AI models and work done outside hours, moral rights waiver
Part C — GDPR obligations, 24-hour breach notification, data retention on termination
